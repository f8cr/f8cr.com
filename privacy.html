Autumn Dispatch

Privacy

Autumn Dispatch is a commercial last-mile navigation tool for an owner-operated delivery vehicle.

What we store

  • Vehicle identification number (VIN)
  • The owner’s Tesla OAuth account id
  • Waypoints the owner submitted
  • Timestamps for those events

What we do not store

  • Tesla passwords
  • Payment cards
  • Sentry or cabin camera media

This data is used only to send an owner-approved trip to the vehicle and to confirm vehicle and charge state for that trip.
