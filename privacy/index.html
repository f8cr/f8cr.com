Batch Address Drop

Privacy

Effective August 15, 2026

Batch Address Drop sends a batch of owner-approved stops to an owner-operated delivery vehicle. It uses Tesla Fleet API only for that owner’s authorized vehicle.

Who this applies to

This policy covers the application and the public origin at korrybusch.com.

What we collect

  • Vehicle identification number (VIN)
  • The owner’s Tesla account identifier from OAuth
  • Waypoints and routes the owner submits
  • Vehicle and charge state needed to plan or confirm a trip
  • Timestamps for those events

What we do not collect

  • Tesla passwords or multi-factor codes
  • Payment cards or bank credentials
  • Sentry, dashcam, or cabin camera media
  • Contacts, messages, or unrelated personal files

How we use it

Data is used only to send an owner-approved trip to the vehicle and to confirm vehicle and charge state for that trip. We do not sell data. We do not use it for advertising. We do not share it with third parties except Tesla, as required to operate Fleet API, and except when the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Application data is stored on systems the operator controls for this application. The Tesla public key published at /.well-known/appspecific/com.tesla.3p.public-key.pem is a public verification key only. The matching private key is not hosted on this domain.

Retention

Trip and vehicle-state records are kept only as long as needed to operate the current delivery work and to diagnose recent failures. They are then deleted. OAuth tokens are kept only while the owner’s authorization remains active.

Your control

The owner can revoke this application in the Tesla account security settings and can remove the virtual key from the vehicle Locks screen. Either action stops further vehicle commands and telemetry for this application.

Children

This application is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way, the updated version will be posted at https://korrybusch.com/privacy with a new effective date.
